# Consolidation Block 15 — C071–C075

Date: 2026-09-28
Scope: Daily field records and evidence

## Accepted builds

- C071 creates project-isolated dated daily records with explicit duplicate policy and append-only draft/review/approval revision history.
- C072 links workforce and activity observations to existing assignment identities while permanently separating observations from approved time, payroll, payable quantities and financial postings.
- C073 links photos and notes through existing custody identities. Retries are idempotent, originals remain authoritative, and upload/privacy failures stay visible.
- C074 preserves manually recorded weather, delivery and constraint observations with source labels. Provider data requires its own record identity and provider outage never removes manual observations.
- C075 preserves approved history through attributable corrections and produces deterministic report counts and fingerprints across workforce, evidence, observations and linked canonical actions.
