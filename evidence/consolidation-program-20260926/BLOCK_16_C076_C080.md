# Consolidation Block 16 — C076–C080

Date: 2026-09-28
Scope: Inspections, corrective actions and reinspection

## Accepted builds

- C076 creates versioned limited-inspection and punch checklist definitions. Only an approved version can be frozen for use, and the contract explicitly denies statutory-certification status.
- C077 records pass, fail and not-applicable results under the assigned inspector role. Required evidence and not-applicable reasons are enforced; incomplete inspections cannot complete.
- C078 creates one deterministic canonical corrective action per failed inspection item. Owner and due date are mandatory, retries are idempotent, and origin links are immutable.
- C079 requires a different authorized reviewer for reinspection, preserves every result, permits closure only after a passing reinspection and restricts reopening to an attributable quality lead.
- C080 reconciles the dated daily record, completed inspection, corrective action and reinspection into deterministic internal/client evidence packages. Client output withholds internal custody identities.
