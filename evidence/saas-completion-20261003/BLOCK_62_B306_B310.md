# Block 62 — B306-B310 — public legal-information path

## Product decision record

- **Problem:** Terms, Privacy and the platform legal notice existed as isolated pages. The notice used a legacy `/disclaimer` identity, legal title/date facts were duplicated, and users had no direct document-to-document path.
- **Decision:** Govern one bilingual legal-document map and effective date, connect every document, expose canonical `/legal-notice`, retain `/disclaimer` compatibility, and bind public/footer/authenticated navigation and accessibility acceptance.
- **Customer effect:** Visitors and signed-in users can reach and move among Terms, Privacy and Legal Notice without authentication, dead ends or ambiguous naming.
- **Content boundary:** The approved legal document text remains unchanged; this block governs identity, discoverability, semantics and route continuity.
- **Operations:** B301-B310 reach the required ten-build publication and authenticated Chrome boundary.
- **Rollback:** Revert B306-B310. No schema, provider configuration or customer data changes are included.

- **B308:** Complete the semantic and bilingual public Privacy path.
