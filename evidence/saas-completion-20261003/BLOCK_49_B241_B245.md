# Block 49 — B241-B245 customer billing history

- B241 reads company-scoped subscription, invoice, credit and dispute records into a secret-safe customer projection.
- B242 exposes the projection only through authenticated financial-administrator or platform-super-administrator authority and derives company scope from the session.
- B243 validates every billing-history identity, amount, status and timestamp at the browser boundary.
- B244 adds bilingual, responsive loading, empty, retry, invoice, credit and dispute states to Billing & Support.
- B245 binds the block to focused acceptance and the five-build push boundary.

The history omits provider customer/session/invoice/dispute references, raw webhook payloads, payload digests and payment-method data. It creates no payment, credit, dispute or database record. B241-B245 are the first five unpublished builds after the accepted B231-B240 publication; the next publication and full authenticated Chrome smoke are due after B246-B250.
