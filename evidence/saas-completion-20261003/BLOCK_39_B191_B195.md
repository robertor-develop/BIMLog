# Block 39 — B191–B195 customer-controlled support closure

Decision: a requester may close only a resolved case and may reopen only a closed case. Reopening requires a 10–1000 character, secret-rejecting reason. Both operations use the authenticated requester identity, company/requester scope, an expected revision, a locked transaction, an immutable event, and administrator notification. Resolution evidence remains attached when a case is reopened.

- B191: strict customer close/reopen intent.
- B192: durable audited lifecycle reason.
- B193: requester-scoped atomic lifecycle route.
- B194: strict browser projection of lifecycle accountability.
- B195: bilingual close/reopen controls, stale-write recovery, and timeline reason.

Publication cadence: first five builds after the B181–B190 publication; push after validation, hold publication until B200.
