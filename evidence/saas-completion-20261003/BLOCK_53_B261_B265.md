# Block 53 — B261-B265 source-bound commercial verification evidence

- B261 creates secret-safe verification evidence tied to one exact source commit and a bounded validity window.
- B262 attaches digest-backed evidence to each live commercial verification result when release identity is bound.
- B263 rejects malformed, contradictory, mismatched or incomplete evidence at the browser boundary.
- B264 shows operators the bound source, validity and abbreviated evidence digest in English and Spanish.
- B265 adds a fail-closed release check that accepts only current verified evidence for the exact candidate source.

The block does not fabricate provider credentials, prices, webhook secrets, portal configuration or sender verification. Production remains honestly blocked until authenticated Stripe and SendGrid resources exist. B261-B265 are the first five builds after the accepted B251-B260 publication and remain unpublished until B270.
