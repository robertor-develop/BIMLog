# Block 45 — B221–B225 durable commercial persistence bridge

This block connects the accepted commercial database authority to bounded server-side persistence contracts. It does not activate Stripe, expose customer checkout, move money, store payment credentials or change production data.

- B221: exact company-scoped persistent reads for subscription, current term, seat ledger and provider binding.
- B222: atomic order and checkout creation with request fingerprints, idempotent replay and conflict rejection.
- B223: signature-verified raw provider receipt persistence with exact SHA-256 payload identity and conflicting-replay rejection.
- B224: paid invoice persistence with subscription, order, completed-checkout and provider-receipt lineage plus the next digest-linked audit event.
- B225: focused acceptance, release evidence and the full exact-head pre-push gate.
