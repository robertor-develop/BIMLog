# Launch Readiness Block 3 — LR011–LR015

## Outcome

The public Pricing journey now distinguishes immediate free signup from paid-plan operational availability without exposing provider configuration, credentials, internal blocker codes, or protected launch evidence. When paid services are not ready, the page truthfully routes the customer to consultation and states that the public page takes no payment. Invalid or unreachable availability fails visibly.

## Builds

- LR011 derives the safe public commercial-availability projection.
- LR012 exposes it through a no-store, nosniff public endpoint.
- LR013 strictly validates the exact browser response and rejects unknown or contradictory shapes.
- LR014 presents bilingual, accessible Pricing guidance for available, consultation-only, and unverifiable states.
- LR015 binds the complete public pricing-availability path to permanent regression coverage.
