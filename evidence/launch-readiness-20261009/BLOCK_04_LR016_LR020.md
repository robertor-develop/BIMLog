# Launch Readiness Block 4 — LR016–LR020

## Outcome

Pricing now uses the verified commercial-availability response to choose the next customer step. Free signup always remains available. Professional, Team, and Business offers route to account creation only when the existing launch authority reports paid plans available; otherwise they preserve the selected plan, billing cycle, and use case into the governed consultation path. Enterprise remains agreement-defined and sales-assisted. No public Pricing action takes payment or grants entitlement.

## Five builds

- LR016 defines one deterministic availability-aware customer journey decision.
- LR017 routes available paid offers into registration with exact commercial intent.
- LR018 fails paid offers safely to consultation when availability is unverified or consultation-only.
- LR019 preserves custom Enterprise and bilingual next-step truth without duplicating checkout or onboarding.
- LR020 binds Pricing, registration, onboarding, and Contact continuity to permanent regression coverage.
